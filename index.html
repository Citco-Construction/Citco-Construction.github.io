Family Owned Since 1983

Built by the Vidal family, for Miami.

Citco Construction, Inc. is a 100% family-owned general contracting firm rooted in Coral Gables. The company operates under the license of Sergio Carlos Vidal, a Certified General Contractor active in the State of Florida since 1983.

Four decades in, the approach hasn't changed: give every commercial and residential client close attention, hold every trade partner to a high standard, and never let a project outrun its plan.

Today Citco focuses primarily on commercial construction across Miami-Dade — office, retail, hospitality, and multifamily — while continuing to take on a curated slate of residential work.
